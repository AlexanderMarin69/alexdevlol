HELP & CONTACT

Support

Need help with Mod Complex? Found a bug, or have a question? I'd love to hear from you.

Contact

Support is handled on the community Discord server: join the Discord and post your question or bug report there.

Please include your operating system, a description of the issue, and steps to reproduce it if possible. A screenshot helps. Avoid sending passwords or sensitive information.

Quick answers

  • Fullscreen. The game starts in fullscreen. Press F or F11 to switch between fullscreen and a window; the window can be resized freely.
  • Settings. Press Esc during a run to open the settings, including sound, display and leaving the run. The run waits while they are open.
  • Sound and screen effect. M turns the music on and off, and C turns the CRT screen effect on and off.
  • Missing Steam achievement. Achievements are saved with your progress and sent to Steam again every time the game starts, so launching the game with Steam running should bring it through.

Purchases and refunds

Purchases and refunds are handled by Steam. See Steam Support.
